Who We Are

Kingsford Media Embedded Finance Co (also known as Kingsford Media API Banking Limited) is a forward-thinking fintech company based in Nsukka, Enugu State. Founded and led by experienced payment gateway engineers, we specialize in delivering robust embedded finance solutions that empower Nigerian businesses to thrive in the digital economy.

Our mission is simple: to make digital payments accessible, secure, and seamless for businesses of all sizes across Nigeria. We understand the unique challenges of the Nigerian market and have built our solutions from the ground up to address them.

With deep technical expertise in payment systems, API integration, and financial technology infrastructure, we serve as your trusted partner in navigating the complex landscape of digital finance.

Comprehensive Fintech Solutions

Payment Gateway Integration

Seamlessly integrate secure payment processing into your website or mobile application. Accept cards, bank transfers, USSD, and mobile money with a single API. Built for Nigerian businesses, optimized for Nigerian banks.

API Banking Solutions

Leverage our robust banking APIs to build custom financial products. Access real-time account information, initiate transfers, verify accounts, and manage transactions programmatically with enterprise-grade security.

Embedded Finance Infrastructure

Embed financial services directly into your platform. From digital wallets to virtual accounts, we provide the technical backbone for businesses looking to offer financial services to their customers.

Payment Gateway Engineering

Custom payment gateway development and optimization services. Whether you need a white-label solution or technical consulting to enhance your existing infrastructure, our engineers deliver scalable, compliant solutions.

Technical Integration Support

Full-service technical support from initial integration through go-live and beyond. Our engineering team ensures smooth implementation, thorough testing, and ongoing optimization of your payment systems.

Compliance & Security Consulting

Navigate CBN regulations, PCI-DSS requirements, and financial technology compliance standards. We help you build secure, compliant systems that protect your business and your customers.
